Episode 4

How to not crash.

Safety Theoretical

A great pilot is defined by their ability to anticipate risks and manage situational awareness. This crucial episode focuses entirely on aviation safety, modern collision avoidance systems, and spatial orientation. We will discuss how to properly interpret instruments during low-visibility scenarios, respond immediately to automated cockpit warnings, and understand the core principles of Crew Resource Management (CRM). Learning how to steer clear of obstacles—both natural and man-made—is the absolute foundation of a long and incident-free career.
