Episode 7

where to find a job as a pilot

Career General

Navigating the aviation job market requires a strategic approach, whether you are a freshly certified commercial pilot or transitioning from military service. In this session, we map out the various career paths available in the industry today. From regional carriers and major international airlines to cargo operations, corporate aviation, and flight instructing, we weigh the pros and cons of each sector. You'll also learn valuable tips on building your logbook hours, networking within the aviation community, and acing the simulator assessment during the interview process.
