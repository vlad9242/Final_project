Episode 8

How to land a plane.

Practical Technical

Landing is widely considered the most challenging and rewarding phase of flight. This comprehensive guide covers the entire approach and touchdown sequence. We will break down how to establish a stabilized approach, intercept the instrument landing system (ILS) glideslope, and configure your aircraft with the correct flaps and landing gear. You will learn how to visually judge the runway environment, execute a perfect flare at just the right moment, and properly deploy thrust reversers and speedbrakes to bring the aircraft to a safe stop.
