Episode 2

How to start a plane.

Practical Technical

Before you can soar through the clouds, you need to know how to bring a multi-ton piece of machinery to life. Starting a commercial airliner isn't as simple as turning a key in an ignition. This episode breaks down the complex overhead panel, the specific sequence for introducing fuel, and how to successfully engage the jet engines. We will walk you through the standard cockpit flow, the auxiliary power unit (APU) startup, and the exact moment to initiate the engine ground start cycle so you are fully prepared before the pushback.
