Episode 3

How to fly a plane.

General Practical

Once you are in the air, understanding the fundamentals of flight mechanics is what keeps you there. In this lesson, we shift our focus from ground operations to basic aerodynamics and manual flight controls. You will learn how to manipulate the yoke and rudder pedals to manage pitch, roll, and yaw. We will cover how to maintain a steady altitude, read your primary flight display (PFD), and execute smooth, coordinated turns. Whether you are adjusting your trim or managing airspeeds, this video lays the groundwork for confident manual handling.
