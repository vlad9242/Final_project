Episode 5

How to take off.

Practical Technical

The transition from the ground to the sky requires precise timing, calculation, and absolute focus. This video guides you step-by-step through the takeoff phase of flight. We will explain how to calculate your critical decision speeds—specifically $V_1$ (decision speed), $V_R$ (rotation speed), and $V_2$ (safety speed). You will learn the exact technique for smoothly advancing the throttles, maintaining centerline tracking down the runway, rotating at the correct angle, and managing flaps and landing gear retraction once a positive rate of climb is established.
