what to do if you're lost.

Safety Practical

Even with the most advanced navigation suites, unexpected instrument failures or severe weather can disorient a pilot. This final episode covers vital emergency navigation protocols and survival skills. We will review the classic aviation rule of thumb: Aviate, Navigate, Communicate. Learn how to utilize cross-checks with ground-based radio beacons (VORs), how to effectively request radar assistance from air traffic control using emergency frequencies, and the exact steps to regain situational awareness and safely re-establish your flight path.
