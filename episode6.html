Episode 6

How to be a professional pilot.

Career Theoretical

Flying the aircraft is only half the job; embodying professionalism, discipline, and regulatory knowledge is the rest. This episode looks past the flight deck controls to explore the daily responsibilities and lifestyle of a commercial airline captain. We dive into standard operating procedures (SOPs), strict adherence to aviation regulations, effective communication with air traffic control (ATC), and the psychological readiness required to command a crew. Discover what airlines look for beyond technical flying skills.
